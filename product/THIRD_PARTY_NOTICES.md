`app/src/Foundation/Arch.lex.tex` adapts the architecture data types from
UOR Foundation's PrismPM `stdlib/src/Foundation/Arch.lex.tex`, commit
`fe5b8b73a94134c316329a8d1b09ebc97b555c5f`.

The original project offers MIT OR Apache-2.0. The copied subset is used under
MIT, whose original notice is retained in `PRISMPM-LICENSE-MIT.txt` beside this
file. Changes: retained only the type declarations through `View`, omitted
upstream demonstration values and later types, and changed the document title
to an available local glossary phrase. The official qualified type ownership
and field shapes are unchanged.

This notice does not establish the provenance or redistribution terms of the
existing v86, BIOS or Linux image files. Those remain separately unresolved in
the product model and generated contract register.
